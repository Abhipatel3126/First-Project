📝 About Student Registration

Welcome to the Student Registration Portal. This webpage has been created to make the student registration process simple, fast, and convenient.

Students can submit their required details through the online registration form. The submitted information will be collected securely through Google Forms.

  • Enter your correct personal details.
  • Provide your correct enrollment number.
  • Enter a valid 10-digit phone number.
  • Select your gender and blood group.
  • Check your information before submitting the form.
